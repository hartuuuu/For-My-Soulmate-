I just want you to know na kahit hindi ako nakaka-reply agad, kahit days pa yung tagal na wala tayong chats, kahit months pa tayong hindi nag-uusap, ikaw pa rin ang best friend ko. Okay lang naman siguro na hindi pa natin feel ’yun, diba? We’re literally just starting our 20s. Hindi naman kailangan na bigla tayong maging super adult just because 20 na tayo HAHAHAHA.

And honestly, I hope hindi mo kailangang maging “adult na adult” agad. You deserve to still be carefree sometimes, to laugh over stupid things, to enjoy the little things, and to just exist without feeling like you have to figure everything out right away. You’re only 20. First step pa lang ’yan sa 20s mo HAHAHAHA.

I hope wala munang masyadong hinanakit at problema na dumating sa’yo ngayong first day mo as a 20-year-old. But I hope kahit papaano, this reminds you na hindi mo kailangang dalhin lahat mag-isa. Kahit parang wala ako minsan, nandito lang ako. Hindi man ako laging present, hindi man ako mabilis mag-reply, at kahit minsan ilang buwan tayong walang matinong usapan, hindi ibig sabihin na nawala na yung friendship natin. Andito pa rin ako. Andito pa rin kami ni Keira. We love you naman eh. 🥹

I hope this year is a little kinder to you. I hope you get more reasons to smile, more moments that make you feel genuinely happy, and more days where your chest doesn’t feel so heavy. I hope you get to experience things that remind you that life isn’t just about all the problems you’re dealing with right now.

And please, don’t pressure yourself too much. Hindi mo kailangang malaman agad kung ano gagawin mo sa buhay. Hindi mo kailangang have everything figured out just because you’re 20 now. We’re all just figuring things out as we go HAHAHAHA. Ako nga rin hindi ko alam ginagawa ko minsan 😭.

Anyway, wala na talaga akong masabi HAHAHAHA. I just really want you to have a happy birthday. I hope you know how much I appreciate having you in my life, even if I don’t always say it. Here’s to another year of you, and hopefully, another year of us being stupid together HAHAHAHA.

Happy 20th birthday, my best friend. I love youuuuu. Please take care of yourself, okay? And remember, kahit gaano pa tayo katagal hindi mag-usap, ikaw pa rin ’yan. Best friend pa rin kita. No expiration date HAHAHAHA. 💗
